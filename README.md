<h1 align="center">Richie</h1>

<p align="center">An AI agent that answers your customers on your website, and knows when to call a person.</p>

## The idea

You add one script tag to your website. Richie appears in a chat widget and talks to your visitors.

Richie answers from your own knowledge base and nothing else. It replies in the visitor's language, refuses questions outside your business, and hands the conversation to your team when it cannot help or when the visitor asks for a person.

Your team works those conversations in a shared inbox. A teammate can take over from Richie at any time, reply, and hand the conversation back. Every answer Richie gives shows which sources it used and why it answered or handed off.

Richie is the agent. The widget, the dashboard, and the inbox are the product around it.
