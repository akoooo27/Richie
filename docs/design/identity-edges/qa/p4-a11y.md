# Identity edges, Phase 4: keyboard and screen-reader semantics (WP4.2)

**Tester:** WP4.2 · **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Standard:** WCAG 2.2 AA, plus the accessibility rules in Part A §4.6–§4.11, the domain specs and AS §4.0 / §4.4 / §7.4
**Scope:** all 73 boards in `.design/edges/project/` (canvas "Richie Identity Edges", assembly `1793888623-5e41`). This is a test report, not a fix: no board, stylesheet or spec was changed.

**Result:** 0 blockers, 4 majors, 13 minors (17 findings). The board markup itself is clean. Every Tab stop has a name and a visible focus style, no focus is trapped or hidden, axe reports no violations, every wrapper matches its base, and the long-email, code-input, brand-mark and label-in-name rules hold on every board. The four majors are about behaviour the boards can't show: a countdown that ticks inside a focused button's name, busy states nothing announces, a status region missing on one board, and time limits with no warning.

---

## 1. Method

1. **Build:** `node .design/qa-rig/build.mjs .design/edges/project .design/edges/out-p4-a11y .design/edges/blobs` → 73 boards, 0 warnings. The fluid variant (`X.html`) is the one tested.
2. **Serve:** `python3 -I -m http.server 8822 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory .design/edges/out-p4-a11y` (port checked free with `lsof` first).
3. **Own audit** (Chromium from the rig's Playwright, scripts kept in `.design/edges/qa/p4-a11y-audit/`). For every board at **1440 × 900 and 390 × 844** (146 runs) it records:
   - the accessibility tree (`page.locator('body').ariaSnapshot()`);
   - the full Tab order. For each stop it records the computed role, name, description and states (CDP `Accessibility.getPartialAXTree`), the visible label text, whether the computed focus style changes (outline, shadow, border, background, colour, decoration), `:focus-visible`, whether the stop sits in a drawn `.is-focused` ring, and whether anything covers it (hit test at the centre and two corners after the browser scrolls it into view). It also flags a stop that sits visually above the stop before it in the same container, and checks whether Tab cycles or escapes;
   - headings, landmarks, live regions (`role=status/alert`, `aria-live`) with their text, every `aria-describedby` (resolved), `aria-labelledby`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-disabled`, `disabled`, `readonly`, `tabindex`, `inert`, dialogs (role, `aria-modal`, name, description), inputs (label, `autocomplete`, `inputmode`, `enterkeyhint`, `required`), images and SVGs not hidden, `target=_blank` links, duplicate ids, `lang`, `<title>`.

   Raw JSON per board is in `.design/edges/qa/p4-a11y-audit/json/`; `digest.txt` is the readable dump.
4. **Wrappers:** the 21 `-mobile` / `-dark` wrappers were diffed against their bases (tree and Tab order, both widths): **all 21 are semantically identical** to their base.
5. **Rig:** `node .design/qa-rig/run.mjs --out .design/edges/out-p4-a11y --port 8823 --viewports 390x844,1440x900 --variant fluid --results .design/edges/qa/p4-a11y-rig`. This gives 73 boards × 6 combos (light and dark at 390, 1440 and 200 % zoom) plus 292 keyboard passes. **0 blockers, 0 majors, 504 minors. axe: 0 violations at any impact.** The minors are all on the known list:
   - 457 targets under 44 px: footer links at 24 px on fine pointers, 36 px sidebar items, 32 px row buttons, inert page buttons behind overlays;
   - 33 axe colour-contrast items marked "needs review": text over the scrim, the outline **Link Microsoft** button. Contrast belongs to the P4 contrast tester;
   - 14 targets under 24 px, all inline links in sentences, which 2.5.8 exempts.
6. **Cross-check against the specs:** for each board I compared the focus target on arrival, which region announces what, `role=alert` only where focus doesn't move, busy and countdown rules, document titles and the long-email markup with Part A §4.6–4.11, AS §4.0 / §4.4 / §7.4 and the board's page in `spec-edge.md`, `spec-settings.md`, `spec-recovery.md` or `spec-sso.md`.
7. **By eye:** rig focus crops (`shots/*/…-focus-first|mid|last.png`) for Main, Recover-code (OTP ring on the active slot), Security-unlink and Signin-redirecting.

**Canvas conventions applied, not reported:**
- `.is-focused` is a drawn arrival ring, not real focus.
- Overlay pages sit inside `inert` with the dialog as a sibling layer.
- Programmatic focus targets (`tabindex="-1"` `h1`, `h2` and alert titles) draw no ring.
- `aria-current="page"` in the settings sidebar is set by the app.
- `<title>` is the board title; the document title comes from the spec.
- Controls the specs say are links only on the canvas are not counted against the board: E11's sign-out buttons (spec-edge §E11) and Main's provider buttons (spec-sso §11 #12).

### What I could not test

- **Real screen readers** (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver macOS and iOS, TalkBack) and **speech input**. Everything said here about announcements comes from the computed accessibility tree, plus known screen-reader behaviour that I flag as unverified where it matters (A11Y-01, A11Y-03).
- **Anything dynamic the boards don't draw:** real focus moves on arrival, after actions and on dialog close; live-region timing; countdown ticks; the "Error: " title prefix; the 150 ms provider-return sequence.
- **OS and browser sheets:** WebAuthn and passkey prompts (including conditional UI from `autocomplete="username webauthn"`), the Google and Microsoft pages, the clipboard API and the "Press Command-C" fallback, one-time-code autofill, the offline service worker.
- **Real document titles** (boards carry board titles), reflow at 400 % zoom, and real mobile keyboards and switch access.
- **Emails** in real mail clients (`Email-recovery-alert` is a drawing; spec-recovery §4.3 says how the real email differs).

## 3. What passed, by area

### Foundation (WP3.1: `Main`, `-mobile`, `-dark`, `Signedout`, `-mobile`, `-dark`, `Security`, `Account-sidebar`, `Profile`)
- **Identity frame:**
  - one `main` and one `contentinfo`, no skip link (Part A §4.7);
  - one `h1#page-title[tabindex=-1]`;
  - the slot is always present with an empty `p[role=status]` and `p[role=alert]` in one cell;
  - footer Richie · Help · Privacy · Terms in the same order everywhere.
- **Sign in:**
  - `Email` is a real label;
  - `type=email`, `autocomplete="username webauthn"`, `inputmode=email`, `autocapitalize=none`, `enterkeyhint=go`;
  - the commit is `type=submit` in a `novalidate` form;
  - the passkey is a `button type=button`.
- **Provider controls:** names equal their visible text ("Sign in with Google", "Sign in with Microsoft"). The G is `<img alt="">` and the Microsoft squares are `svg aria-hidden`.
- **Settings shell:**
  - the skip link is the first stop at every width and targets `#page-title` (`tabindex=-1`);
  - `nav "Account"` is the only landmark outside `main`;
  - every section is a region named by its `h2`; Passkeys, Google and Microsoft, and Where you're signed in are `tabindex=-1` focus targets;
  - row actions start with their label ("Rename the Pixel 8 passkey", "Unlink Google", "Link Microsoft", "Sign out of Safari on iPhone"), and the popup ones carry `aria-haspopup="dialog"`;
  - **Change email** is described by the address, read as one string.
- **Signed out:** the note link "Sign-in and security" is in a sentence (inline, 2.5.8 exempt).
- No board-level findings.

### Edge pages (WP3.2, 17 boards)
- **Outcome pages** (E1, E4–E6, E8, E10, E13, E14): `h1` focus target, badge `aria-hidden`, a slot only where an action reports (E4, E8, E10, E11). None on E1, E5, E6, E13 or E14, as spec-edge §3.4 says.
- **E1:** the email in the lede uses the §4.8 markup (`ra-sr` full address plus `aria-hidden` halves, `translate="no"`) and reads as one string.

  - **Copy** is named "Copy reference";
  - the slot sits under the reference (D5);
  - "contact support" carries ", opens in a new tab";
  - the reference reads as one string.
- **E7:** the failure is in `role=alert` with focus kept on **Send code** (drawn), and the email is kept (3.3.7).
- **E9:** the countdown is `aria-disabled`, stays focusable and is never `disabled`; the reason is `role=status` (D10).

- Findings: A11Y-01 and A11Y-06 on `Signin-limited`; A11Y-01 on `Limited`; A11Y-05 on the E2 drawer; A11Y-04 and A11Y-03 at spec level.

### Settings gaps (WP3.3, 15 boards)
- **Dialogs:** `role=dialog`/`alertdialog`, `aria-modal`, `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby` resolve, and each is a `<form>`. Initial focus matches AS §7.4: the field on rename and change email, Cancel on the unlink confirm, the code input on step 2, and Remove passkey after the failure.
- **Field errors** (`Security-rename-error-mobile`, `Profile-email-taken`): `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby` to the error, which has no role. The reserved slot keeps an empty `role=alert` for action failures. The messages say what to do ("Use 40 characters or fewer.", "…Use a different one."), and the typed value is kept (3.3.7).
- **Hidden ghosts:** the step-2 ghost pane is `inert`; busy ghosts ("Unlinking…", "Saving…") don't leak into names.
- **Status lines:**
  - added (`role=status`, focus stays on **Add a passkey**);
  - linked (polite);
  - link error (`role=alert`, focus on the `h2`, so it is allowed by the AS rule);
  - remove failure (`role=alert` inside the confirm, focus on **Remove passkey**);
  - email changed (polite, focus drawn back on **Change email**).
- **Code input on `Profile-email-code-mobile`:**
  - one name, "Email code, 8 letters and numbers";
  - slots `aria-hidden`;
  - `autocomplete="one-time-code"`, no auto-submit;
  - described by the meta;
  - the new-code line also in an sr `role=status`.
- Findings: A11Y-02, -13 (`Security-passkey-limit`), -14 (`Security-load-error`), -15 (`Security-link-error`), -05 on the seven dialog, drawer and sheet boards at < 768.

### Recovery (WP3.4, 18 boards)
- **Frame:** every page has the identity frame. R2–R5 forms use the right tokens:
  - R2 `autocomplete=username`;
  - R3 `email`;
  - R4 `one-time-code`;
  - R5 `name` and `off`, with `maxlength` 100 / 200 / 1,000.
- **R5:**
  - hints linked by `aria-describedby`;
  - required fields `required`, optional ones say "(optional)";
  - `Recover-limited` keeps every typed value (3.3.7) and describes the unavailable commit by the reason (`aria-describedby="limit-status"`).
- **Status pages:** facts are a real `dl` (term and definition pairs). Addresses read as one string; the masked one reads "j, hidden, at rivera.studio" (Part A §4.8). Every "Contact support" link says ", opens in a new tab". R9's "Start a new request on November 12" is `aria-disabled` and focusable.
- **R1:** the same three methods as Sign in, plus a real `h2` "Lost access to your email?".
- **3.3.8:** R5's required questions are name and devices, which support reviews by hand. Passkey, Google and Microsoft remain alternatives on R1, so the request isn't a cognitive-function gate.
- **The email drawing:** `lang="en"`, mascot `alt="Richie"` on purpose (spec-recovery §4.3), one button, and the link fallback.
- Findings: A11Y-07, -09, -10, -11, and A11Y-04 at spec level.

### Google and Microsoft in sign-in (WP3.5, 14 boards)
- **P1:** the busy Google control is a `button` with `aria-disabled` (never `disabled`), a gerund label and the spinner `aria-hidden`. The other methods stay enabled.
- **P2 and P3:**
  - the alert has no role, and its title is the `tabindex=-1` arrival target (Part A §4.10);
  - P3's **Copy** is named "Copy reference" and is the first stop after the alert title;
  - the alert sits before the form in DOM and visual order.
- **A4:**
  - `h1` "Welcome back, Jamie" with the name `translate="no"`;
  - the chip address is one string;
  - "Not you?" has the name "Not you? Forget this account on this device", which starts with the visible label.
- **A2 and A3:**
  - `given-name`, `family-name` and `email` tokens;
  - hints by `aria-describedby`;
  - the commit is described by the legal line (spec-sso §5.1);
  - the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links are inline in a sentence (2.5.8 exempt);
  - A3's names are prefilled from the provider (3.3.7).
- **P5:** the chip shows the provider's initials and the email only; "Use a passkey instead" is available.
- **P6:**
  - one-name code input with hidden slots;
  - "Use a passkey instead" and "Can't get the code?" alternatives (3.3.8);
  - **Link Microsoft** commit;
  - Cancel is a link.
- **P4 and P7:** `h1` targets, no live region (spec-sso §8.4).
- Findings: A11Y-03 (drawn on `Signin-redirecting`), -08, -12, -16, -17.
