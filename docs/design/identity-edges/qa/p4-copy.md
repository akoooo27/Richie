# P4 copy review (WP4.4)

Scope: the visible text of all 73 boards in `.design/edges/project/*.dc.html` (35 are thin wrappers that `dc-import` a base, so their copy is the base's), plus every copy table, state string and email in `spec.md` (Parts A and B), `spec-edge.md`, `spec-settings.md`, `spec-recovery.md` and `spec-sso.md`. Method: per-board text extracted to scratch files (screen-reader-only text and `aria-hidden` ghost/busy strings listed separately), then rule checks, cross-area comparison, board-versus-spec comparison, and an editor's read. No board, stylesheet or spec was edited.

## Result in one paragraph

The boards themselves are clean. Across all 73 there is no placeholder, no `{variable}`, no straight quote or apostrophe, no `...`, no "!" outside a sample customer message, no "invalid/oops/session/sign-in code/verification code/log in", no ALL-CAPS label (the only `text-transform: uppercase` is on avatar initials and code slots), no "→" or "·" in visible text, no workspace term, and the Help / Privacy / Terms footer is identical on every standalone identity page. Board-versus-spec drift is one string (COPY-07). The real problems are in the **spec-only copy**, where four designers wrote the same things in parallel: the failure line for a pressed provider button, the security emails (two specs define the "provider linked" email, three variants of fine print, serial commas, time zones), the Microsoft organization-approval message, and the limit sentences. Those need PM rulings, not four independent fixes.

Counts: **31 findings. Major 6, minor 12, polish 13.** By owner: PM 12, WP3.4 recovery 8, WP3.3 settings 4, WP3.5 SSO 4, WP3.2 edge 3.

## Board versus spec

All 73 boards were compared with the spec tables for their page and state. Every visible string matches its table (word for word, including punctuation) except one: `Security-recovered`, Google row (COPY-07). The wrappers (`-mobile`, `-dark`, `Security-*` overlays, `Profile-*` overlays) import their base, so their copy is the base’s. The copy-only states (E1a, E1b, E2 restore, E3, E7 variants, E9 variants, E10 variants, S1–S10 slots, R1 variants, R2–R12 error rows, A2, A3, P1–P7 states) were read in the specs; each defines every `{variable}` it uses (`{email}`, `{ways}`, `{phrase}`, `{client}`, `{when, where}`), and none contains a TBD or placeholder. The one soft placeholder is the "2 business days" promise (COPY-12).

## Strongest copy (keep as is)

- `Session-expired`: "Your changes on this page aren’t saved yet. Sign in again within 30 minutes and we’ll put them back." Says what, a real number, a promise.
- `Timeout` lede and `Offline`: "Start again with jamie.rivera@… to get a new code." and "We’ll load the page as soon as you’re back online."
- `Error`: "It’s a problem on our side, not with your account." Reassures without apologizing.
- `Error-link` note: "Waiting on a request? We’ll email you each time its status changes."
- `Signedout-prompt`/`Signedout-everywhere`: what stops, what stays ("Your passkeys and settings stay as they are.", "Apps you’ve connected keep their access…").
- `Signin-provider-canceled`: "Google didn’t share anything with Richie. You can try again or sign in another way."
- `Signin-remembered-provider`: "Last time on this device, you signed in with Google."
- `Provider-no-email` and `Provider-no-account`: the h1 states the cause; the note gives the third reader the one fact they need.
- `Recover-approved`: the date is the headline; "We wait 3 days so your old address can cancel if someone else asked." explains the wait in the reader’s interest.
- `Recover-cancel`: "Not sure? Cancel it. Nothing about your account changes."
- `Recover-details` hints ("Roughly is fine, like “last Tuesday”.") and the anti-phishing line "Richie support never asks for email codes."
- `Security-remove-failed` and `Security-unlink`: each confirm says what stops and what still works.
- The R11 email (`Email-recovery-alert`): subject, masked address, decision before the button, reassurance after.
- The one-name discipline in visible copy: no "session", no "sign-in code", consistent "Send a new code", "That code isn’t right. You have 3 tries left."
